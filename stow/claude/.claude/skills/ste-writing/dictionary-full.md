Entry format, one line per head word:
- `word (pos) → ALTERNATIVE (pos)` — the word is NOT approved in STE; replace it with the UPPERCASE alternative. `[brackets]` give the sense an alternative applies to, or an instruction when no single replacement exists.
- `WORD (pos) = meaning` — the UPPERCASE word is approved, but only with the stated meaning; `other meanings:` lists what to use instead for different senses.
